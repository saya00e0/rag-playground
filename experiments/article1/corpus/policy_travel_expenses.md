# Harborline Travel and Expense Policy

Fictional evaluation document; it describes no real organization, person, or product.

## Airfare

Economy class is required for flights shorter than six hours. Premium economy is permitted for flights of six hours or longer. Business class requires written approval from a vice president regardless of flight length.

## Lodging

Hotel costs are reimbursed up to 180 USD per night in standard cities and up to 260 USD per night in high-cost cities. The high-cost cities are London, New York, Tokyo, Zurich, and San Francisco.

## Meals

A daily meal allowance of 65 USD applies. On a travel day with a departure before 07:00 or a return after 21:00, the allowance for that day increases to 80 USD.

## Submission

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the trip's end date. A report submitted later requires approval from the finance director and is reimbursed at 80 percent of the approved amount.
