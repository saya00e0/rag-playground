# Harborline Customer Data Retention Policy

Fictional evaluation document; it describes no real organization, person, or product.

## Retention periods

Customer support transcripts are retained for 18 months after the ticket is closed and are then deleted. Billing records are retained for seven years to meet tax obligations. Application logs that contain customer identifiers are retained for 30 days; logs with identifiers removed may be kept for 13 months for capacity analysis.

## Account closure

When a customer closes their account, profile data is deleted within 45 days of closure. Billing records are exempt and follow the seven-year rule.

## Legal holds

A legal hold overrides every deletion schedule in this policy until the legal department releases the hold.
