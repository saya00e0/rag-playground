# Data Processing Agreement between Harborline Inc. and Northwind Analytics

Fictional evaluation document; it describes no real organization, person, or product.

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") forms part of the services contract between Harborline Inc. ("Controller") and Northwind Analytics ("Processor") and governs the processing of personal data by the Processor on behalf of the Controller.

## 1. Definitions

"Personal data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person that the Processor processes on behalf of the Controller. "Processing" means any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, analysis, transfer, and deletion. "Subprocessor" means any third party engaged by the Processor to process personal data under this Agreement. "Personal data breach" means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to personal data. "Business day" means a day other than a Saturday, a Sunday, or a public holiday in the Controller's place of registration.

## 2. Scope and Purpose

The Processor processes personal data only to provide the product analytics services described in the services contract. The categories of data subjects are the Controller's end users and the Controller's employees who administer the analytics service. The categories of personal data are account identifiers, usage events, device information, and approximate location at city level. The Processor must not process special categories of personal data and must not combine the Controller's data with data from any other customer.

## 3. Processor Obligations

The Processor processes personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller. If the Processor believes that an instruction infringes applicable data protection law, it must inform the Controller within 5 business days and may suspend that instruction until the Controller confirms or changes it. The Processor ensures that every person authorized to process the personal data is bound by confidentiality. The Processor assists the Controller in responding to data subject requests and must forward any such request it receives directly within 3 business days.

## 4. Subprocessors

The Controller grants general authorization for the Processor to engage the subprocessors listed in Annex 2. The Processor must give the Controller at least 30 days' written notice before engaging any new subprocessor or replacing an existing one. The Controller may object to the change in writing within 14 days of receiving the notice. If the Controller objects and the parties cannot agree on a solution within 30 days of the objection, the Controller may terminate the affected services without penalty. The Processor remains fully liable for the performance of every subprocessor.

## 5. Security Measures

The Processor encrypts personal data at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Access to production systems requires multi-factor authentication and is reviewed every 90 days. The Processor performs an external penetration test at least once every 12 months and shares the executive summary of the report with the Controller on request. Backups containing personal data are encrypted and are retained for no longer than 35 days.

## 6. Personal Data Breach Notification

The Processor must notify the Controller of a personal data breach without undue delay and in any case no later than 36 hours after becoming aware of it. The initial notification must describe the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects concerned, and the measures taken or proposed. The Processor must deliver a written follow-up report within 10 days of the initial notification, describing the root cause and the remediation completed. The Processor must not notify data subjects or supervisory authorities about the breach without the Controller's prior written consent, unless required by law.

## 7. Audits

The Controller may audit the Processor's compliance with this Agreement once in any 12-month period, giving at least 30 days' written notice. In addition, the Controller may carry out an extra audit within 60 days after any personal data breach, with 10 days' notice. Audits are performed during business hours and at the Controller's cost, except that the Processor bears the cost of an audit that reveals a material breach of this Agreement.

## 8. International Transfers

The Processor must not transfer personal data outside the European Economic Area without the Controller's prior written approval. Any approved transfer must be covered by the standard contractual clauses attached as Annex 3. The Processor must inform the Controller within 30 days if a change in local law in a destination country could prevent it from complying with those clauses.

## 9. Return and Deletion of Data

When the services contract ends, the Processor must, at the Controller's written request made within 30 days of termination, return all personal data in a commonly used machine-readable format. Whether or not the data is returned, the Processor must delete all personal data, including copies held by subprocessors, within 60 days of termination, and must provide the Controller with a written certificate of deletion signed by an officer of the Processor. Backups are deleted when they expire under the 35-day retention rule in section 5.

## 10. Liability

Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid by the Controller in the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. The limit does not apply to a breach of section 6 or section 8, for which liability is limited to three times that amount.

## 11. Term and Termination

This Agreement remains in force for as long as the Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller. Either party may terminate this Agreement for material breach if the breach is not cured within 30 days of written notice. Sections 6, 9, and 10 survive termination.
