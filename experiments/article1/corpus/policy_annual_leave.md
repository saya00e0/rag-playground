# Harborline Annual Leave Policy

Fictional evaluation document; it describes no real organization, person, or product.

## Accrual

Employees accrue 1.5 days of annual leave for each full month of service, up to a maximum of 18 days per calendar year.

## Carry-over

Up to five unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire if they are not taken by March 31. Unused days beyond those five are forfeited on December 31 and are not paid out.

## Requests

Leave of three or more consecutive working days must be requested at least ten working days in advance. Shorter leave requires two working days' notice. Parental leave is covered by a separate policy.
