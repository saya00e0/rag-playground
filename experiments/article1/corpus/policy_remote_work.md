# Harborline Remote Work Policy

Fictional evaluation document; it describes no real organization, person, or product.

## Eligibility

Full-time employees who have completed their six-month probationary period may work remotely for up to three days per week with their manager's approval. Employees who are still on probation must work on site on every scheduled working day.

## Extended arrangements

A request for four or more remote days per week requires approval from both the employee's manager and the department director. Extended arrangements are reviewed every 90 days and lapse automatically if the review is not completed.

## Contractors

Contractors are not covered by this policy. Remote work for contractors is governed by the terms of their individual service agreement, and contractor requests are approved by the vendor management office.

## Equipment

Employees working remotely must use a company-issued laptop connected through the corporate VPN.
