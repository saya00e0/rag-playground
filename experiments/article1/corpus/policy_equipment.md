# Harborline Equipment Refresh Policy

Fictional evaluation document; it describes no real organization, person, or product.

## Laptops

Laptops become eligible for replacement after 36 months in service. Engineers whose laptops have less than 16 GB of memory may request early replacement after 24 months in service. Damaged laptops are repaired first; replacement is considered only if the repair is not economical.

## Lost or stolen devices

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security desk within 4 hours of discovery. The employee then receives a loaner device within one business day.

## Peripherals

Monitors, keyboards, and docking stations are replaced only when they fail.
